Questions

Before you ask

Who owns my book?

You do. Your manuscripts, drafts, and finished files are yours — and they are never used to train AI models.

What do I get at the end?

Real files you can publish with: a Word document (DOCX), an EPUB for ebook stores, and a print-ready interior PDF in 6×9 or 5.5×8.5 — plus a standard submission format.

I already wrote a draft. Can I start there?

Yes. Upload your manuscript and the Studio drafts your Book DNA from the text itself and assesses where it stands. Completion packages carry it the rest of the way.

Will the AI make things up?

It cites only the sources you give it — and flags claims it cannot back instead of dressing them up with invented citations. You review and approve every change.

Do I have to use AI drafting?

No. There are editing-only and formatting-only paths, and clearly labeled simulated drafts for rehearsing before you spend a credit.

How do I get my book into stores?

You upload it yourself, guided step by step — the Studio prepares everything and walks you through it. Our free author guide lays out the whole road before you pay a dollar.
