How it works

Start where you are

Bring an idea, or bring a manuscript you already wrote. Either way, the path ends at finished files you own.

1 · Plan it

Every book gets a profile and a writing DNA — audience, purpose, voice, and structure — so every draft sounds like you and stays on course.

2 · Write it

Draft chapter by chapter with AI, in sections, with your sources cited honestly and every version kept. Edit in a real formatted editor; review AI changes one by one and accept only what you want.

3 · Finish it

AI finishing passes (proofread, line edit, developmental), cover concepts, and typesetting for submission or print — 6×9 or 5.5×8.5 — with an export check before anything is called production-ready.

Already have a manuscript?

Upload it. The Studio reads it in, drafts your book’s DNA from the text itself, and assesses where it stands — then a completion package carries it the rest of the way: passes, cover, and finished files.

The rules of the house

  • You decide. AI suggests; nothing changes without your approval.
  • Your words stay yours. Manuscripts are never used to train AI models.
  • No invented sources. The Studio cites only sources you give it — and flags claims it cannot back.
  • Every version kept. Nothing is ever overwritten or lost.

Questions

Before you ask

The things every author asks first.

Who owns my book?

You do. Your manuscripts, drafts, and finished files are yours — and they are never used to train AI models.

What do I get at the end?

Real files you can publish with: a Word document (DOCX), an EPUB for ebook stores, and print-ready PDFs in 6×9 or 5.5×8.5 — plus a standard submission format.

I already wrote a draft. Can I start there?

Yes. Upload your manuscript and the Studio drafts your book’s DNA from the text itself and assesses where it stands. Completion packages carry it the rest of the way.

Will the AI make things up?

It cites only the sources you give it — and flags claims it cannot back instead of dressing them up with invented citations. You review and approve every change.

Do I have to use AI drafting?

No. There are editing-only and formatting-only paths, and clearly labeled simulated drafts for rehearsing before you spend a credit.

How do I get in?

The Studio is in private beta, by invitation, while we finish testing. Email us about your book — the “Ask about the beta” button is the door for now. Public checkout opens at launch.
