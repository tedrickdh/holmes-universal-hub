How it works

Start where you are

Bring an idea, or bring a manuscript you already wrote. Either way, the path ends at finished files you own.

1 · Plan it

Every book gets a profile and a writing DNA — audience, purpose, voice, and structure — so every draft sounds like you and stays on course.

2 · Write it

Draft chapter by chapter with AI, in sections, with your sources cited honestly and every version kept. Edit in a real formatted editor; review AI changes one by one and accept only what you want.

3 · Finish it

AI finishing passes (proofread, line edit, developmental), cover concepts, and typesetting for submission or print — 6×9 or 5.5×8.5 — with an export check before anything is called production-ready.

Already have a manuscript?

Upload it. The Studio reads it in, drafts your book’s DNA from the text itself, and assesses where it stands — then a completion package carries it the rest of the way: passes, cover, and finished files.

The rules of the house

  • You decide. AI suggests; nothing changes without your approval.
  • Your words stay yours. Manuscripts are never used to train AI models.
  • No invented sources. The Studio cites only sources you give it — and flags claims it cannot back.
  • Every version kept. Nothing is ever overwritten or lost.
