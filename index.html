About

The founder

Tedrick Holmes is a Houston educator, author, and builder. He holds a Master of Education, teaches 7th-grade science in Aldine ISD, and serves as Chief Operating Officer of an ABA services practice — while writing books and building the digital projects gathered here.

The work looks varied from the outside. From the inside it is one thread: teaching, storytelling, and building useful things for families and neighborhoods — including the family and community history of Houston's Kashmere Gardens and Haverstock, and education projects still ahead, like Houston L.E.A.D. Preparatory Academy.

More ventures in development under this roof include the learning tools LessonForge, BehaviorBridge, Science Quest, and Au-Some Voice AAC, and the OMNIX personal AI project. As each one is ready, it will appear here — at its own subdomain, under this same domain.
