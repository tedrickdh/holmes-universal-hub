About

About the founder

Tedrick Holmes is a Houston educator, author, and builder. He holds a Master of Education, teaches 7th-grade science in Aldine ISD, and serves as Chief Operating Officer of an ABA services practice — while writing books and building the digital projects gathered on this site.

The idea behind Holmes Universal Solutions is simple: one home, honestly organized. When a new project starts, it gets a room here — a page or a subdomain at holmesuniversal.com — not a whole new house across town.
