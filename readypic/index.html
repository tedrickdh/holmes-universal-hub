ReadyPic · PhotoPass

Pictures worth keeping, kept well.

The photography and image venture of Holmes Universal Solutions — because a family's history lives in its photographs.

Why ReadyPic exists

readypic.org · readypic.holmesuniversal.com

Every family and every neighborhood has photographs scattered across phones, boxes, and old hard drives — the birthday, the block, the house that isn't there anymore. ReadyPic (also known as PhotoPass) is the project built to bring those pictures together and make them easy to find, organize, and share.

It sits naturally inside Holmes Universal: the same company that documents Houston family and community history treats photographs as what they are — the archive itself.

Visit readypic.org

In One Line

Capture it. Keep it. Find it again.

ReadyPic is being built so the picture you took five years ago is as easy to pull up as the one you took five minutes ago — and so the people you share it with can actually enjoy it.
